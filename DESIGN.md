# Portfolio design

Cinematic engineering studio: deep black #08090b, warm white #f2eee7, cinnabar #df503e, brass #c4a46a. Chakra Petch carries the identity and headings; Manrope carries body text. Fine dividers and angled actions connect the armor to readable project stories.

## Surface

1. A pinned 96-frame sequence: the builder, the architecture, the result. Scroll controls the camera pullback, armor separation, rotation, and reassembly.
2. Three alternating project features with original code-native product illustrations.
3. A native expandable archive, a concise biography and capabilities, and a direct email contact.

## Reference

The built-in image generator supplied docs/work-concept.png. Its initial character-based hero concept was rejected by the generation tool. The hero now uses the complete supplied CadNav IronMan.obj armor model, replacing the earlier partial-model reconstruction. Source colors inform physically based red, gold, and graphite materials, with emissive eyes and reactor and rigidly posed arms; the source MAX rig is not imported. Only rendered imagery is shipped. Work-section layout, palette, typography, and content hierarchy follow the selected concept; product studies intentionally replace the concept's fabricated interface data with verified workflow descriptions.

The 19 September 2026 model replacement passed render and browser verification; see docs/QA.md.

## Accessibility and resilience

All content is server rendered. Links, archive, and navigation work without animation. Reduced-motion users get the poster and a short page. Decoded frame memory is bounded; the poster and textual content stay usable if image fetching or canvas support fails. Keyboard outlines remain outside angled buttons, and compact screens use a non-sticky hero.
