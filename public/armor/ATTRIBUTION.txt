Armor sequence credits

The complete armor geometry comes from the supplied IronMan.obj model
in the CadNav Model_D0901A13 archive: https://www.cadnav.com/

The renderer preserves 140,755 armor faces after removing 9,072 helper-ring
faces. Source colors inform the physically based red, gold, and graphite
materials; the eyes and reactor use emission. Arms are posed with rigid
transforms. The source MAX rig is not imported.

Website materials, lighting, camera movement, animation, and renders were
prepared for Tejas Das's portfolio. Only the rendered imagery is distributed
with the website. The original downloadable model and rig remain outside
the Git repository and are not distributed here.

The source archive's terms permit modifications and use as part of artwork,
projects, and websites, with attribution to cadnav.com. Standalone resale
or redistribution of the source model is prohibited by those terms.

Iron Man is a Marvel character. This is an independently made fan-inspired
portfolio presentation and is not affiliated with Marvel.
