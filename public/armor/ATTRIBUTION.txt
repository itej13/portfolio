Armor sequence credits

Base helmet, forearm, and hand geometry is derived from an Iron Man model
downloaded from CadNav: https://www.cadnav.com/

The rest of the suit is a local reference-guided reconstruction. Website
materials, lighting, camera movement, animation, and renders were prepared
for Tejas Das's portfolio. These rendered images are part of the website.
The original downloadable 3D model is not distributed here.

The source archive's terms permit modifications and use as part of artwork,
projects, and websites, with attribution to cadnav.com. Standalone resale
or redistribution of the source model is prohibited by those terms.

Iron Man is a Marvel character. This is an independently made fan-inspired
portfolio presentation and is not affiliated with Marvel.
